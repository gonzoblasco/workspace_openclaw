# Workflow: Narrative Content

## What it does

Planning and writing of narrative content: novels, stories, worldbuilding, characters.

## Phases

### 1. Premise
Central idea, genre, tone, target audience, main conflict.

### 2. Structure
Narrative arc, chapters, characters (character sheets), worldbuilding (rules, lore, geography).

### 3. Writing
Writing narrative content (chapters, scenes, dialogue, descriptions). Assisted co-writing.

### 4. Review
Narrative coherence, character consistency, pacing, tone, plot holes.

### 5. Export
DOCX, EPUB, Markdown, depending on destination. Final formatting.

## Outputs
- Story outline
- Character sheets
- Worldbuilding bible
- Written chapters
- Exportable files (DOCX, EPUB, MD)

## When to use
- When starting to write a novel or story
- When needing to structure an existing story
- When wanting to export narrative content to a publishable format
