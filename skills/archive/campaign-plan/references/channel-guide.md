# Channel Selection Guide — Campaign Plan Reference

Guía para seleccionar canales según objetivo y audiencia.

## Owned Channels

| Channel | Best For | Metrics | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blog/Website | SEO, thought leadership | Traffic, conversions | Medium |
| Email | Nurture, retention | Open rate, CTR | Low-Medium |
| Social (organic) | Awareness, community | Engagement, reach | Medium |
| Webinars | Education, lead gen | Registrations, pipeline | High |

## Earned Channels

| Channel | Best For | Metrics | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| PR/Media | Awareness, launches | Coverage, share of voice | High |
| Guest content | Audience expansion | Referral traffic, backlinks | Medium |
| Influencer/Partner | Trust, reach | Engagement, conversions | Medium-High |
| Community | Awareness, feedback | Mentions, engagement | Medium |

## Paid Channels

| Channel | Best For | Metrics | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search ads (SEM) | High-intent capture | CPC, CTR, CPA | Medium |
| Social ads | Awareness, retargeting | CPM, CPC, ROAS | Medium |
| Display/Programmatic | Awareness | Impressions, CPM | Low-Medium |
| Sponsored content | Thought leadership | Engagement, leads | Medium |
