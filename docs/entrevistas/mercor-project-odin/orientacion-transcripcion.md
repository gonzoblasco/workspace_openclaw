Welcome to Project Odin! You will record yourself doing meaningful professional
work, explaining your decisions as you go. Your screen and narration should show
how an expert turns a realistic problem into a finished deliverable. Start with
work you know. Think about a normal week and choose a task where your knowledge
and judgment matter. A strong task has a clear goal, substantive work, and a useful
result. Complexity comes from the decisions you make, not from adding extra
steps. For your first task, aim for recording of about 15 to 20 minutes. If
your task has a lot of steps, you can split it into two tasks. Shorter tasks
are easier to get right in one recording take, which saves you effort. Three apps
are preferred when they contribute meaningfully. Fewer including one are
allowed if the task is reasonably complex. Every task must demonstrate
professional skill and judgment. Opening extra apps does not establish
complexity. Input files are optional. If you use them, prepare them before
recording and follow the privacy, licensing, and disclosure requirements.
Eligible inputs may be prepared with Gemini before the session. A Gemini
watermark on a prepared input is allowed. Other watermarks are not. Do not show or
interact with AI tools during recording or use AI to generate or revise task
content. The workflow has four stages. Propose your idea, prepare the approved
task, record it, and submit your files for review. First, propose your idea in
studio. Describe your goal, starting point, apps, and expected result. Wait for
approval before recording. If your plan changes materially, request the change
and wait for approval again. Check the current app categories. Band apps cannot
be used for new work. Limited use apps must follow the below 20% rule and any
stricter conditions, including background-only restrictions. Apps with
alternatives preferred remain allowed. Chrome and Safari are allowed. Firefox is
preferred. Unrestricted apps need no separate app approval or catalog entry.
If an app is missing in studio, choose other and enter its exact name. Your idea
still needs approval. Once your idea is approved, keep working in the same studio
task. Its status changes to in progress. Studio fills in your full approved idea
for you. Copy only the goal section, word for word, into three places. Task
recorder, your opening document, and the request section of metadata.rtf. Then
complete metadata.rtf. List each app in the order you use it, listing an app again
each time you return to it and add your approved goal and your task steps. When
you start your recording, remember to hide the approval ID. Prepare your files
and recording environment. Launch your apps, finish sign-in and loading, open the
required files, then minimize the apps. Keep private information hidden. Start
with only the goal document visible behind task recorder. Begin recording,
minimize task recorder, and say, "I will be beginning my task soon." Here is the
workflow I have in mind. Keep the introduction under 20 seconds. Minimize
or close the goal document, bring your prepared apps forward, and begin the
task. Narration is the most important part of your recording. Record the
complete task in one take. Explain what you're doing, where and why, as though
teaching a colleague. For example, I'll click the blue export button in the top
right corner to save the report. Name the controls you use and explain the
decisions behind each meaningful step. Use your cursor to point out relevant
content and move deliberately. Important note, before you press record, make sure
no personal information is visible. Close personal tabs, emails, and notifications.
Keep one clear active window or arrange needed windows side-by-side. Keep the
menu bar, clock, and dock visible. Avoid stacked ordinary windows and full-screen
mode. Temporary dialogues within the same app may overlap while you actively use
them. Minimize or close them when finished. This exception does not allow
private information to appear. Avoid continuous typing, mouse inactivity, or
audio silence longer than 45 seconds. Break long entries into shorter sections,
then review, point, explain, or take another meaningful action. Keep narration
connected to the work. After recording, upload four files in studio, video.mp4,
the original task recorder zip, deliverables.zip, and metadata.rtf. Keep the
original zip intact and put metadata.rtf in its own field. Run both AutoQC
checks. Fix or dispute every major flag before submitting. Minor flags are
advice and do not block submission. Fix them when practical. Inspect the flagged
section before deciding what to fix. A file problem may need a corrected upload.
A recording problem may require recording again in the same task. Never edit the
production recording. If a flag is wrong, explain why and include time stamps.
We run the checks after fixing issues, complete the checklists honestly, and
click submit for review. Saving a loan does not submit your task. For help, ask
Maven first. If unresolved, forward the complete thread to Odin ideation for
tasking questions or Odin help desk for technical and onboarding issues. Include
your task link, current step, expected result, actual result, and a screenshot
when useful. If guidance conflicts or you remain stuck, ask for escalation in the
thread. Support team will route it to the appropriate EPM. Start with a strong
idea, follow the approved workflow, and make your expertise clear. Welcome to
Odin.
