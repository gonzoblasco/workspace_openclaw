# Kanam OS: ADRs as the Backbone of AI Reliability

## Post 5: ADRs Powering AI System Reliability

ADR (Architecture Decision Record) files are the cornerstone of Kanam OS's reliability and maintainability. Each ADR documents a key architectural choice, its rationale, and its implications - creating a living record that guides future development.

### Why ADRs Matter

- **Traceability** - Every major decision is documented, making it easy to understand why the system works the way it does.
- **Consistency** - ADRs ensure that the architecture remains coherent as the system evolves.
- **Onboarding** - New team members can quickly grasp the system's design by reading the ADR history.

### The ADR Philosophy

ADRs follow a strict format: title, context, decision, consequences, and implementation details. This structure ensures that each record is self-contained and actionable. By treating ADRs as the primary source of architectural truth, Kanam OS maintains a reliable, auditable, and scalable system.
