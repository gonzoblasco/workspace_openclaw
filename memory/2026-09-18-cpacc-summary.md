# CPACC Study Summary (Extracted from Video Transcripts)

## Exam Structure & Weighting
- 80 % of the exam focuses on the **human side of accessibility** (disabilities, assistive technologies, models of disability).
- 40 % covers **understanding disabilities** and the technologies people use.
- 40 % covers **principles of universal design** and inclusive design.
- 20 % covers **technical standards, regulations and management**.
- 100 multiple‑choice questions, 2 h to complete, passing score = 600 / 800.

## Disability Models to Master
- **Medical model** – disability as an individual medical condition.
- **Social model** – disability created by inaccessible environments.
- **Biopsychosocial model** – intersection of biological, psychological, and social factors.
- **Economic model** – cost of support and accommodations.
- **Functional‑solution model** – focus on practical solutions (e.g., adaptive devices, assistive tech).
- **Social‑identity model** – cultural affiliation (e.g., Deaf community).
- **Charity model** – pitfalls of paternalistic assistance.

## Test‑Day Checklist
- Government‑issued photo ID (name must match registration).
- No phones, smartwatches, notes, books, or second monitor.
- Private, quiet room with stable internet (if remote proctor).
- Only pre‑approved accommodations are allowed.
- Strictly no communication during the exam (any breach → dismissal).

## Practical Tips
- **Hands‑on practice**: build a small accessible component (HTML + ARIA) to reinforce concepts.
- **Use the BoK as a map**: tick off each competency as you feel confident.
- **Combine resources**: watch Deque videos, then read the corresponding Book of Knowledge sections.
- **Review mock exams**: analyse every wrong answer to understand reasoning.
- **Schedule breaks**: the exam is 2 h; practice timed sections to build stamina.
