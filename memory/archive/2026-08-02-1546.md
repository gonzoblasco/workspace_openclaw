> Did an automated contrast pass on the two components reported. Here are the measurements and proposed fixes:
>
> ## 1. Pagination dots — two separate failures
>
> ### 1a. Inactive dot contrast (WCAG 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast)
>
> The inactive dots use `--color-neutral` (`rgba(5, 54, 89, 0.1)` in light, `rgba(223, 226, 229, 0.2)` in dark) as their background. Because it's translucent, the effective color depends on what's behind it:
>
> | Mode | Blended color | Against body bg | Ratio | Needed |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | Light | `#D9E1E7` | `#F1F4F7` | **1.20:1** | 3:1 |
> | Dark | `#3A3B3C` | `#111112` | **1.68:1** | 3:1 |
>
> Both fail the 3:1 threshold for non-text UI components.
>
> ### 1b. Use of color (WCAG 1.4.1 Use of Color)
>
> The only visual difference between active and inactive dots is a color change (neutral → accent). There is no secondary cue — no size, shape, border, or text difference. This is a use-of-color violation: users who cannot perceive the color difference have no way to identify the current page.
>
> **Proposed fix:**
> - Use `--color-text-secondary` (`#4E606F` light / `#AAAFB5` dark) for inactive dots instead of `--color-neutral`. That gives 5.90:1 (light) and 8.55:1 (dark) against body — well above 3:1.
> - Add a secondary non-color cue for the active dot: either a border ring, increased size, or an `aria-current="page"` visible indicator. The `aria-current` attribute is already set in the code, but it's not visually styled.
>
> ## 2. Badge "New" — translucent background on colored surfaces
>
> The non-semantic badge variants use translucent backgrounds (`--color-background-*` at `0x33` alpha). On a white surface these all pass comfortably (7-10:1). But when a badge is placed on a colored card background, the translucent fill blends with the card color and the text contrast becomes unpredictable.
>
> Example with the green variant (`--color-background-green` = `#24BB5E33` + `--color-text-green` = `#09441F`):
>
> | Background | Blended badge bg | Ratio | Status |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | White `#FFFFFF` | `#D3F1DF` | 9.37:1 | Pass |
> | Body `#F1F4F7` | `#C8E9D8` | 8.67:1 | Pass |
> | Card from screenshot `#686D57` | `#5A7D58` | **2.43:1** | Fail |
>
> The root cause is that `0x33` (20% opacity) backgrounds were designed for white surfaces, but the design system's own showcase places badges on colored cards. The fix should either:
>
> 1. Increase the alpha to `0x4D` (~30%) or higher for non-semantic badge backgrounds, or
> 2. Add a `@media (forced-colors: active)` fallback and document that non-semantic badge variants are only guaranteed on `--color-background-surface` or `--color-background-body`, or
> 3. For badges on colored surfaces, use the semantic variants (neutral, info, success) which have solid backgrounds and are not affected by the underlying surface.
>
> Happy to open a PR for the pagination dots fix. The badge issue needs a design decision on whether to bump the alpha or constrain usage.
>
