The defect: rounding at each line and at discount/tax intermediate steps. Fix: only round the final total.

Also "descuento se aplica sobre el subtotal exacto" — so subtotal exact (sum of qty*unit without rounding). Tax on subtotal minus discount (exact). Only final rounding to 2 decimals.

So:

```python
def invoice_total(lines, discount_pct, tax_pct):
    subtotal = 0.0
    for qty, unit in lines:
        subtotal += qty * unit
    discount = subtotal * discount_pct / 100
    tax = (subtotal - discount) * tax_pct / 100
    return round(subtotal - discount + tax, 2)
```

That's it.
