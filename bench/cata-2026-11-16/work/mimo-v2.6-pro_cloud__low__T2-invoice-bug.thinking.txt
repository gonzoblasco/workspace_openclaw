The defect: rounding at intermediate steps (line, discount, tax) instead of only at the final total. Fix: compute exactly, round only at the end.

Also `subtotal += line` where line is rounded — remove rounding. Keep float arithmetic (or use exact). The spec says only final rounding to 2 decimals allowed.

So:

```python
def invoice_total(lines, discount_pct, tax_pct):
    subtotal = 0.0
    for qty, unit in lines:
        subtotal += qty * unit
    discount = subtotal * discount_pct / 100
    tax = (subtotal - discount) * tax_pct / 100
    return round(subtotal - discount + tax, 2)
```

That's it.
